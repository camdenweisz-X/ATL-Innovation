Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

CanItWait helps renters report apartment maintenance problems with a photo, get a suggested urgency level, and send a clear request to their property manager. It is built by The InnovAItors, a student team in the ATL Cup 2026, and is currently free and in testing. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password. Passwords are stored in hashed form by our database provider; we never see them. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google.
  • Profile: your name, an optional phone number, and whether you want email updates.
  • Properties and units: for managers, property names, addresses and after-hours phone numbers. For residents, the properties you join and your unit. If you send requests to a landlord who doesn't use CanItWait, the contact name, email or phone you enter for them.
  • Maintenance requests: photos, descriptions, the urgency level, category, answers to follow-up questions, entry permission, pet and availability notes, status updates, and messages between you and your manager.
  • Usage counts: how many urgency checks you run, so we can limit abuse.
  • Basic technical data: our hosting provider keeps short-lived server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and troubleshooting.

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. Your browser stores your sign-in session, theme choice and unsent drafts on your own device.

How we use it

  • To run your account and sign you in.
  • To suggest an urgency level, safety steps and a drafted request from your photo and description.
  • To deliver your request to the managers of your property, and to show status updates and messages to both of you.
  • To send email notifications, if you leave them turned on.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

Who can see your information

  • Residents see only their own requests.
  • Managers see requests sent to properties they manage, along with the resident's name, unit and contact details needed to schedule the repair.
  • Access is enforced in the database itself, not just in the app.

Services we use

We rely on these providers to run CanItWait. They process data on our behalf and only for these purposes:

  • Supabase stores accounts, requests and photos (United States).
  • Vercel hosts the website and its server functions.
  • Google Gemini analyzes the photo and description when you tap Check urgency. We use Google's free tier, under which Google may use what is sent to improve its products. Please don't include faces, documents or other sensitive personal details in photos or descriptions.
  • Google Sign-In, if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Resend sends email notifications, when they are enabled.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, we use only your name, email address and profile picture, and only to create and identify your account. We don't use Google user data for advertising, don't sell it, and don't use it to train AI models. CanItWait's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account. That permanently removes your account, profile, requests and photos, and any property where you are the only manager. Messages you sent on someone else's request stay in that request's history without your name attached. If the project ends, we will delete the data.

Your choices

  • View and edit your profile and places in Settings.
  • Turn email notifications on or off in Settings.
  • Delete your account in Settings, or ask us to do it.
  • Email us for a copy of your data.

Security

Connections are encrypted, access rules are enforced by the database, and our server keys are kept out of the app's code. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't upload anything you would not want a property manager to see.

Children

CanItWait is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
