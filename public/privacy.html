1.Who we are

CanItWait (“CanItWait,” “we,” “us”) is a maintenance reporting service built and operated by The InnovAItors, a student team in the ATL Cup 2026. CanItWait helps renters report maintenance problems with a photo, get a suggested urgency level, and send a clear request to their property manager, and helps property managers receive and track those requests.

CanItWait is currently free and in a testing phase. This policy covers our website and web app at atl-innovation.vercel.app and any related services that link to it.

3.How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To suggest an urgency level, safety steps and a drafted request from your photo and description.
  • To deliver requests to the managers of your property and show status updates and messages to both sides.
  • To send email notifications, if you leave them turned on.
  • To show property managers summaries of their own requests, such as repeat problems and repair times.
  • To prevent abuse, enforce our Terms of Service, fix problems and improve the service.
  • To comply with law and respond to valid legal requests.

CanItWait does not make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. The urgency level is a suggestion: residents choose what to send, and managers decide how to respond.

4.How we share information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral or targeted advertising. We have not done so in the past 12 months. We disclose information only as follows:

  • With your property's managers. Requests you send to a property, along with your name, unit and contact details, are visible to the managers of that property so they can schedule the repair.
  • With service providers who process data on our behalf under contract and only for the purposes in this policy:
    • Supabase: database, accounts and photo and video storage
    • Vercel: website hosting and server functions
    • Google: Gemini AI analysis and, if you choose it, Google sign-in
    • Resend: email notifications, when enabled
    • Twilio: emergency text messages to managers and backup contacts, when enabled
    • Your browser's push service (for example Google, Apple or Mozilla): phone and browser notifications, if you turn them on
  • For legal reasons, when we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law or needed to protect the safety, rights or property of any person.
  • If the project changes hands, for example if CanItWait becomes part of a company. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

When you email or text a landlord who isn't on CanItWait, the message is sent from your own email or messaging app. We don't send it, and that delivery is covered by your provider's privacy terms.

5.AI processing

When you tap Check urgency, your photo, description and the current time are sent to Google's Gemini AI service to produce a suggested urgency level, safety steps and a draft request. We currently use Google's free tier, under which Google may retain this content and use it to improve its products, which can include review by people at Google. Please don't include sensitive personal details in photos or descriptions. When you tap Translate, the text of that request and its messages is sent to Gemini the same way. Videos are not sent to the AI; a single still image from a video is, when there is no photo.

We do not use your content to train our own AI models.

6.Google user data

If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name, email address and profile picture, and we use them only to create and identify your account. We do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it, do not allow humans to read it except as needed for security or legal reasons or with your permission, and do not use it to develop or train AI models.

CanItWait's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7.Cookies and tracking

We don't use advertising, analytics or third-party tracking cookies. Your browser's local storage keeps your sign-in session, your theme choice and any unsent request drafts on your own device, so the app works as expected.

Because we don't track you across websites, there is nothing to opt out of. We also honor browser privacy signals such as Global Privacy Control as an opt-out of any sale or sharing, should our practices ever change.

8.How long we keep it

  • Account and profile: until you delete your account.
  • Requests, photos, videos and messages: until the resident deletes their account or the property is deleted. Messages you sent on someone else's request stay in that request's history without your name attached.
  • Usage counts and server logs: only as long as needed for security and abuse prevention.

When you delete your account in Settings → Delete account, we permanently delete your account, profile, requests, photos and videos, and any property where you are the only manager. Copies may remain briefly in our providers' systems before they are removed. If the project ends, we will delete the data we hold.

9.Your privacy rights

Several states give their residents specific privacy rights. To keep things simple and fair, we offer the following rights to every user in the United States, whether or not your state's law requires it:

  • Know and access: ask what personal information we have about you and get a copy.
  • Correct: fix inaccurate information. You can edit your profile and homes in Settings at any time.
  • Delete: delete your account in Settings, or ask us to delete your information.
  • Portability: receive your information in a common, machine-readable format.
  • Opt out: of the sale or sharing of personal information, targeted advertising and profiling. We don't do any of these.
  • Limit use of sensitive information: we already use it only to provide the service.
  • Non-discrimination: we will not treat you differently for exercising any of these rights.

How to make a request

Email camdenweisz@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject “Privacy request.” We may ask for information to verify your identity. You can use an authorized agent; we may ask the agent for proof of your permission and confirm your identity with you directly. We will respond within 45 days, or tell you if we need up to 45 more days.

Appeals

If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our decision with the subject “Privacy appeal.” We will respond within 60 days and explain our reasons. If you are not satisfied, you may contact your state attorney general.

11.Security

Connections to CanItWait are encrypted. Access rules are enforced in the database itself, so residents can see only their own requests and managers only their own properties, and our server keys are kept out of the app's code. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and any authorities as required by applicable law.

12.Children

CanItWait is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act. Users from 13 to 17 may use CanItWait only with a parent or guardian's permission. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

13.Where data is stored

CanItWait is offered to users in the United States, and we store data with our providers in the United States.

14.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will post the new version here and update the “Last updated” date. For significant changes, we will also let you know in the app before they take effect.
